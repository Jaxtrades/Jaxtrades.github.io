    How it works

    1. Start from the local market. Each suburb's median sale price describes a typical home there (for houses around here, that's roughly 4 bed, 2 bath, 2 car on about 450 m²).
    2. Use real sales if you have them. Recently sold homes nearby are the best evidence of what buyers will pay. Look them up on realestate.com.au and enter them in step 2.
    3. Adjust for differences. Each sale (or the suburb median) is adjusted up or down for bedrooms, bathrooms, car spaces, land size, age, condition and pool, and sales needing the smallest adjustments count most.
