Our story

Built by women who were tired of gritting their teeth through it.

Cozzy started with a leaking microwavable rice sock and a group chat full of "there has to be a better way." We tried every heating pad on the market — cords too short, covers too scratchy, heat that faded in ten minutes. So we spent a year building the one we actually wanted: cordless, genuinely soft, and warm enough to matter.

Today Cozzy is in the bags, beds, and desk drawers of thousands of women who refuse to let a bad day win.

Why heat actually helps

Calms the nervous system

Simply put — warm and cozy feels good, and that matters too.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How long does the battery last?
A full charge gives you 2+ hours of continuous heat on the low setting, less on higher settings. A full charge from empty takes about 2 hours over USB-C.
Is it safe to fall asleep with?
Yes — Cozzy automatically shuts off after 90 minutes of continuous use, so you can doze off without worrying.
Can I wash the cover?
Yep. The plush cover unzips off the heating unit and is machine washable on a gentle, cold cycle. Never submerge the internal heating unit itself.
What colors does it come in?
Terracotta, Blush, Sage, and Midnight. The Duo Bundle lets you mix and match two colors.
What's your return policy?

Do you ship internationally?
We currently ship within the US, with more countries coming soon. Sign up for our newsletter to be the first to know.
