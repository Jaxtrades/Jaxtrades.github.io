    How it works

    1. Start with real sales. Homes that recently sold nearby are the best evidence of what buyers will pay.
    2. Adjust for differences. If a sale had one more bathroom than your home, its price is adjusted down; if it was older or in worse shape, it's adjusted up. Size, bedrooms, bathrooms, age, condition, garage, and pool are all considered.
    3. Weight the closest matches. The final value is a weighted average — sales that needed the smallest adjustments are trusted the most.
